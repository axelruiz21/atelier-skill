# Duty Cycle — concept note

Axel Ruiz studio · 2026

A building is a calendar with walls. Maintenance OS opens that calendar
as a sheet: one seed cuts a hall through a plan, partitions the wings
by recursive binary space division, and hangs a door tree back onto the
hall so every room has a way to the core. The mechanical room is the
cell nearest the centroid. Air, power, water, and fire leave the hall
as parallel risers and reach each served room along that door tree.

The register is not a mood. At the opening of the duty year every asset
draws a residual life from a Weibull distribution — shape by service,
scale shortened by occupancy — from its own mulberry32 stream. The day
control is a sliding aperture of the horizon across that fixed calendar.
Orders inside the aperture are corrective. A crew, sized by the
operator, takes the highest priority first: criticality of the room
divided by days remaining. What the crew cannot take stays open, and
only the open orders carry the accent. Preventive marks are a second,
quieter cadence, phased on each service interval.

The same seed, the same plant, the same day, the same crew: the same
sheet, exactly. Reroll does not stir the drawing; it only chooses the
next integer to stand in the doorway. The export is an edition of that
sheet, named with its seed, the way a work order is named with the
plant it belongs to.

Controls: rooms, plant age, duty day, horizon, occupancy, crew, service
shift, preventive marks, room names, seed. Export at 2× as
`duty-cycle_seed-NNNN.png`.
