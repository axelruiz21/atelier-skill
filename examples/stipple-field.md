# Stipple Field — concept note

Axel Ruiz studio · 2026

A photograph arrives as a continuous surface and leaves as a census.
The instrument reads luminance as demand for ink, throws seeded points
at that demand, and then lets the points argue: each one is pulled, pass
after pass, to the density-weighted centre of the territory it governs.
This is Lloyd relaxation over a weighted Voronoi partition — Secord's
stippling, computed exactly, pixel by pixel — and what it produces is
not a filter but a settlement. Darkness is carried entirely by spacing
and weight; nothing is shaded, everything is counted.

The process is the work. The same seed yields the same field, dot for
dot, because composition never touches unseeded chance — rerolling
walks seed-space with a deterministic step, so exploration is
navigation, not gambling. The seed is printed on the placard and
carried in the filename of every export, the way an edition number is
carried on a print.

When no photograph is offered, the instrument opens on a procedural
study — a lit sphere on a shadowed ground, the draughtsman's oldest
test object — so the tool is never exhibited empty.

Controls: dot count (500–16,000), contrast (a slope on the luminance
curve), relaxation passes (0–8, from grain to blue-noise), dot size,
inverted ink, seed. Export at 2× as `stipple-field_seed-NNNN.png`.
